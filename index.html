Privacy Policy

Effective: October 5, 2026 · Last updated: October 5, 2026

Developer: OneTwinty Games · Contact: onetwintygames@gmail.com

Summary

Kanche works offline. We (the developer) do not collect, sell or share your personal data, and we run no servers that store it.

What is stored on your device

Your player name, coins, levels and stars, items, settings and simple counters (wins, shots, marbles claimed) are stored in the app's private storage on your phone so the Game remembers your progress.

On-device play log (optional)

If Settings → Keep play stats on this phone is on, the Game keeps a short log of its own events (for example "level started" or "level won", last 200 events) on your device. It never leaves your device. Turn it off at any time.

Android backup

If Android backup is on for your Google Account, Android may copy the Game's save (only the save, not the app itself) to your Google Account backup and restore it when you reinstall. This is done by Google under the Google Privacy Policy; the developer cannot see it. You control it in Android Settings → Google → Backup.

Google Play Games (optional sign-in)

If you sign in with Google Play Games, the Game receives your Play Games player ID and display name to show who is signed in, and saves your progress to Google Play Games "Saved Games" in your Google Account. That data is stored and processed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy; the developer does not receive your email address and does not copy your data to any server of its own. You can play without signing in.

Advertising

This version shows no ads and contains no advertising or analytics SDKs. If ads are added later (for example Google AdMob after a win), this policy will be updated before that release to explain what ad partners collect, such as the advertising ID.

Third-party services

The Game runs on Android and uses Google Play services (Android backup, Google Play Games). Their use of data is covered by Google's own policies.

Deleting your data

  • On this phone: Settings → Your data → Delete my data (immediate), or uninstall the Game.
  • Android backup: Android Settings → Google → Backup → manage backups.
  • Cloud save: Settings → Cloud save → Delete cloud save (immediate), or remove the Game from your Google Play Games account (Play Games app/settings → Your data).
  • Or email us and we will explain the steps within 30 days. Because we store nothing on our own servers, there is nothing else to delete.
