Privacy Policy

Effective: October 5, 2026 · Last updated: October 24, 2026
Developer: OneTwinty Games · Contact: onetwintygames@gmail.com

Summary

Single-player Kanche works offline and needs no account. We (the developer) do not collect, sell or share your personal data, and we run no servers of our own that store it. Two features need the internet and involve other parties: the Multiplayer mode relays your display name and chat messages to the other player through a third-party message-relay service, and Advertising uses Google AdMob, which may collect data such as your advertising ID. Both are detailed below.

What is stored on your device

Your player name, coins, levels and stars, items, settings and simple counters (wins, shots, marbles claimed) are stored in the app's private storage on your phone so the Game remembers your progress.

On-device play log (optional)

If Settings → Keep play stats on this phone is on, the Game keeps a short log of its own events (for example "level started" or "level won", last 200 events) on your device. It never leaves your device. Turn it off at any time.

Android backup

If Android backup is on for your Google Account, Android may copy the Game's save (only the save, not the app itself) to your Google Account backup and restore it when you reinstall. This is done by Google under the Google Privacy Policy; the developer cannot see it. You control it in Android Settings → Google → Backup.

Google Play Games (optional sign-in)

If you sign in with Google Play Games, the Game receives your Play Games player ID and display name to show who is signed in, and saves your progress to Google Play Games "Saved Games" in your Google Account. That data is stored and processed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy; the developer does not receive your email address and does not copy your data to any server of its own. You can play without signing in.

Multiplayer

Multiplayer matches need an internet or local network connection. To find and connect you with another player, and to keep the match in sync, the Game sends your chosen display name, quick/typed chat messages, and basic match data (such as scores and turn order) through a real-time message-relay service over the internet. We do not store or read this data ourselves, and it is not used for anything beyond running the match. Players can remove multiplayer chat with the Typed messages setting, which restricts chat to preset quick messages only. You can play the Game entirely without ever using multiplayer.

Advertising

The Game shows ads (banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded-video ads) served through Google AdMob to support free play. AdMob and its partners may collect data such as your advertising ID, device information, and coarse engagement data to serve and measure ads, as described in Google's advertising policy. You can buy the optional ad-removal item to stop interstitial and banner ads. Analytics in the Game itself (see "On-device play log" above) stays on your device and is not an advertising or analytics SDK.

Third-party services

The Game runs on Android and uses Google Play services (Android backup, Google Play Games, Google Play Billing for purchases, Google AdMob for ads, and a third-party message-relay service for multiplayer). Their use of data is covered by their own policies, linked above.

Deleting your data

  • On this phone: Settings → Your data → Delete my data (immediate), or uninstall the Game.
  • Android backup: Android Settings → Google → Backup → manage backups.
  • Cloud save: Settings → Cloud save → Delete cloud save (immediate), or remove the Game from your Google Play Games account (Play Games app/settings → Your data).
  • Or email us and we will explain the steps within 30 days. Because we store nothing on our own servers, there is nothing else to delete.
